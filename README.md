# 38 verified stories, 9 regions: US tariffs on 14 partners, Pakistan first astronaut, Hodeidah strikes

One-page newspaper for Wednesday 25 November 2026: 3 leads, 10 features and
25 briefs, all verified against same-day reporting (Reuters, AP, BBC, UEFA,
Xinhua). Every story is dated 25 November 2026; a DATE CHECK box flags the
four dates readers most often mix up.

## Leads

- **US tariffs hit 14 partners** — Trump proposes new Section 301 duties on
  14 trading partners; China faces the highest rate at 20%.
- **Pakistan first in orbit** — Shenzhou-24 lifts off 08:18 Beijing time and
  docks with Tiangong at 11:56, carrying Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first
  Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign visitor to Tiangong.
- **Saudi jets hit Hodeidah** — the Saudi-led coalition strikes the Yemeni
  port after an evacuation warning; the Houthis threaten Jeddah.

## Features

Dow rebounds as tech sinks; CORY BOOKER takes on Nvidia; four tankers hit by
mines in Hormuz (IRGC claim); OPEC blames the EU for the COP31 fail; 112
drones hit Odesa; Liverpool and Barca win big in the Champions League; 30
dead in Niger State raids; Africa debt can fuel growth; Cairo ring road fire
kills 21; judges urge: drop Case 4000.

## How it is built

`entry.png` (1024x1024) is drawn by `gen.mjs` (kept outside this repo) with a
hand-made 5x7 bitmap font and a self-written PNG encoder — no vector fonts,
no raster images. `main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
and validates the PNG (magic, size, dimensions, IEND, CRC-32).
